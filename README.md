# 🌍 Access to Drinking Water Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes global access to drinking water using data on population size, urbanisation, water-service levels, and geographic regions.

The analysis explores how access to drinking water varies across countries and investigates changes in access between different years, population groups, and regions.

The project was completed using **Google Sheets / Microsoft Excel**, with data cleaning, transformation, statistical analysis, summary tables, and data visualization.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

* Understand and prepare the drinking-water dataset for analysis
* Investigate population size and urbanisation
* Compare access to drinking water across national, rural, and urban populations
* Examine different levels of water service
* Investigate changes in water access over time
* Explore differences across geographic regions
* Calculate summary statistics and percentage differences
* Create visualizations to communicate findings

## 🔎 Analysis Performed

### 1. Population Analysis

Population data was examined to understand the size and distribution of the populations represented in the dataset.

The analysis also considered the relationship between national population and the proportion of the population living in urban areas.

### 2. Water Access by Area

Water access was compared across three population categories:

* National
* Rural
* Urban

The analysis considered four water-service levels:

* At least basic service
* Limited service
* Unimproved service
* Surface service

### 3. Water Access and Population Size

The project investigates whether water-service access varies with population size.

100% stacked visualizations were used to compare the different water-service levels across population sizes.

### 4. Water Access and Urbanisation

Urban and rural population shares were analyzed alongside water-service levels to investigate possible relationships between urbanisation and access to drinking water.

### 5. Change Over Time

The dataset was analyzed across different years to examine changes in water access.

Annual change calculations were included for national, rural, and urban access.

### 6. Regional Analysis

Countries were grouped into geographic regions to allow comparisons of water access and population characteristics across different parts of the world.

---

## 📈 Key Findings

The analysis highlights differences in drinking-water access between countries, population groups, and regions.

Some of the main observations include:

* Access to basic water services varies considerably between countries.
* Urban and rural populations can experience different levels of access.
* Water-service levels can differ substantially across countries with different population sizes.
* Changes in access between years can be positive, negative, or minimal depending on the country and population group.
* Regional differences provide additional context for understanding global water-access inequalities.

> **Note:** The findings above summarize the general patterns investigated in the project. Specific numerical conclusions should be interpreted from the workbook's calculations and visualizations.
